# Design System: Optimism Blog

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Practical guides in the Optimism identity"**

This is a scoped record of the implemented blog extension: `blog/index.html`, its two article pages, `assets/blog.css`, `assets/blog.js`, the catalogue SVG illustrations and instructional evidence in `assets/blog/`, generated by `scripts/generate-blog.py`. It does not define or redesign the homepage. The description names the established direction; it introduces no new visual identity. Product context is in `docs/blog-product-context.md`; the specific editorial composition belongs in `docs/blog-surface-brief.txt` and the first-body-node `BLOG-READ-2026 / ARTICLE-INSTRUCTIONS-2026` comments on the articles.

The existing logo, Joanna type, navy and cyan anchor a light, readable editorial surface. Large regular-weight catalogue headings and compact article titles introduce practical material; muted summaries and small metadata support scanning. TexTerra informs editorial mechanics within this identity. Catalogue diagrams introduce the subjects. Instructional screenshots have captions identifying their source or teaching-example status; neither represents invented client results. This documentation records source behavior after the finish review; it does not claim fresh browser testing or user research.

**Key Characteristics:**

- White reading ground, navy structure, blue interactive text and restrained cyan accents.
- Regular Joanna headings, generous article leading and a bounded reading column.
- Open editorial entries with rounded illustrations, rather than enclosed card panels.
- Search and reading aids progressively enhance complete static articles.

## Colors

### Primary

**Deep navy (`navy`)** anchors the footer, selected topics, submit button and the funnel illustration. **Action blue (`blue`)** distinguishes links, markers, reading progress and active navigation. **Bright cyan (`cyan`)** supplies illustration emphasis and text selection; it is not the ordinary reading-text color.

### Neutral

**Navy ink (`ink`)** is the default text. **Slate blue (`muted`)** supports summaries and metadata. **Pale blue (`pale`)** sets off takeaways and selected interaction surfaces. **Blue-gray rule (`line`)** divides sections and outlines quiet controls. **White paper (`paper`)** is the main reading background. The frontmatter owns these primitives; small state-specific shades stay in component CSS in the sidecar.

**The Reading Ground Rule.** Keep the long-form reading column on white; use pale panels for bounded supporting material.

## Typography

Joanna Sans Nova is self-hosted as the CSS family `Joanna`, in regular and bold weights (400 and 700), with Arial and sans-serif fallbacks. Both headings and body copy use Joanna. Bold is reserved for emphasis and table headers; headings are regular. SVG diagram labels use Arial locally for portability; that is not a second site display family. Code uses the browser's monospace stack.

The frontmatter records desktop base roles. The catalogue display is fluid above its responsive overrides; article titles use a compact fixed-size responsive hierarchy. Story headings use balanced wrapping and a slightly tight fit. Article subsections use a smaller heading (27px, 1.25 leading); navigation and controls sit mostly between 14px and 17px. There is no mathematical global type scale in the CSS.

At widths up to 999px, article titles become 46px and section headings 34px. At widths up to 650px, the catalogue heading is 42px, article title 36px with 1.12 leading and -.025em tracking, section heading 30px, subsection heading 25px, and reading text 19px with unchanged 1.65 leading. At widths up to 360px, the catalogue heading is 37px and article title 32px. The article summary is 21px on desktop and 18px on mobile. The final instruction-revision rules override the earlier article-title declarations.

**The Quiet Heading Rule.** Keep Joanna headings regular and use size, whitespace and placement for hierarchy before adding weight.

## Layout

The blog shell, header and footer align to a centered maximum width (1320px). The fluid side gutter is `clamp(24px,4.6vw,80px)`, overridden to 22px at 650px and 16px at 360px. Spacing is contextual rather than an invented universal scale.

The catalogue has an introductory two-column arrangement, visible search and topic controls, then an open two-column story grid (1.2fr / 1fr, 40px gap). At widths up to 999px the introduction stacks, search occupies a full row and stories become equal columns. At widths up to 650px stories form one column. A single search result has its own compact image-and-copy layout, which also stacks on mobile. At desktop widths from 1000px, the two-result covers are equal height (270px) and contain the full diagram; image-specific backing colors fill remaining space. Other cover contexts use aspect ratios and may crop.

Articles begin with a compact text-only title, summary, metadata and reading tools; decorative article covers are removed while catalogue and related-entry covers remain. The opening is bounded to 1050px, with a 29ch title and 66ch summary. The reading grid pairs contents (250px), gap (64px), and text (up to 760px). At widths up to 1150px the contents column narrows to 215px and gap to 40px. At widths up to 999px the sidebar moves above the text; the reading container remains bounded to 760px.

Contents stay sticky on desktop (top 116px), with their own bounded scrolling area. Below 1000px they stay sticky beneath the header: top 76px on tablet and 72px at widths up to 650px, with a white wrapper below the header in stacking order. Expanded contents navigation scrolls within 50dvh. The header minimum height is 88px, reduced to 76px and 72px at these breakpoints. Root scroll padding is 105px, reduced to 88px on mobile; article heading targets add margins of 16px on desktop, 85px on tablet and 80px on mobile. These are source offsets, not a promise that expanded navigation has a fixed height.

Wide tables scroll inside their own rounded wrapper; they retain a minimum width (500px) rather than shrinking cells beyond legibility. Inline code and long links can wrap. Print removes navigation, the image dialog, reading tools and related content, switches to black text, and uses a single reading column.

## Elevation & Depth

The blog uses flat surfaces, thin dividing rules, tonal contrast and rounded illustration frames. There are no resting or hover card shadows. The search field's focused one-pixel blue box-shadow reinforces its border; it is an interaction indicator, not elevation. A visible three-pixel blue outline with five-pixel offset serves ordinary keyboard focus. The field uses its enclosing focus-within treatment instead of an inner input outline.

**The Flat Surface Rule.** Separate editorial content with spacing and rules; reserve the existing box-shadow for search focus.

## Shapes

Catalogue illustration frames retain their soft desktop and mobile cover corners. Instructional screenshot links use a separate modest radius (12px); the image dialog uses 14px corners and its close button 7px. Search uses a rounded rectangle; topics and the header contact link are pills. Takeaways, quotes, table wrappers and code blocks use bounded rounded surfaces. Article entries themselves have no enclosing border or background. Directional icons are small stroke SVGs; checklist ticks are CSS strokes, not text glyph icons.

## Components

### Search and topic filters

Search combines a visible label, stroke icon, flexible text input, conditional clear control and navy submit button. Submit hover uses a lighter navy-blue. The field changes its border and gains a matching focus ring. A topic's `aria-pressed` state switches from an outlined neutral pill to solid navy with white text; unselected hover uses pale blue.

Typing filters after a short delay (160ms). Query and topic combine; the URL stores both, browser history restores them, and a session-scoped return link preserves them when navigating back from an article. Search includes title, summary, topics, article text and authored aliases. Results and empty-state updates use a polite live region. The empty state provides a real reset action. Without JavaScript, the articles remain visible and a note explains that search requires JavaScript; topic filters stay hidden.

### Editorial entries and illustrations

Each entry groups one image, linked heading, topic/date/reading time and short summary. The image duplicates the destination of the heading, so its decorative link is excluded from the keyboard and accessibility tree. Heading hover changes to blue and moves its arrow slightly; hovering a story link gently scales the illustration (1.025). The scale transition is 650ms with `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; arrow motion is 250ms. Reduced-motion preference removes transitions and image scaling.

The two original diagrams share the navy/cyan/light-blue vocabulary: a conversion path and an illustrative search result. Instructional figures have descriptive alternative text and explanatory captions; catalogue diagrams are not repeated as article heroes. Keep asset-specific illustrative details separate from reusable UI tokens.

### Navigation and actions

The blog header uses the existing SVG logo and plain text links; the active destination has a blue underline. Its outlined contact link turns navy on hover and is hidden below 1000px. At widths up to 650px JavaScript exposes a menu control with `aria-expanded`; Escape closes it and returns focus. Without JavaScript the links remain available. The footer supplies a navy closing surface and standard contact links.

The reset action is a plain blue text button. Article copy and print actions use compact underlined text; clipboard success or failure appears in a polite status message. These actions are exposed only after enhancement. Their behavior is defined in the shipped JavaScript, not by the static sidecar previews.

### Contents and reading support

Contents use native `details` and anchor links. JavaScript opens the contents by default from 1000px and collapses them below that threshold. The active section has a pale-blue background and blue text. Choosing an anchor on a smaller screen closes contents before focusing and scrolling to the heading. A thin blue progress indicator (3px) follows article position; it indicates scroll progress, not verified completion.

Takeaways are pale rounded panels (mobile padding 22px and text 19px); quotes are similarly quiet. Tables use pale headers and thin rules, with tabular numerals. Checklists use blue ticks and row dividers. Related material reuses the editorial entry pattern. The static article body, headings and native contents remain usable without enhancement.


### Instructional evidence and downloads

Screenshots sit in the reading column, inside a thin outlined image link with a white background and zoom-in cursor. Muted captions (15px, 14px on mobile) explain what to inspect and identify the source or teaching example. Source attribution remains a real link where supplied. Documentation examples are not client results.

A normal source-image link works without JavaScript. Enhancement opens a native modal dialog with an original-image link, autofocus close button, caption and keyboard-focusable scrolling image region. The image stays at original size without a maximum-width constraint; its viewport is bounded to 55dvh. The dialog is at most 1160px wide, leaves 16px around the viewport and uses a translucent navy backdrop. Escape uses native dismissal; closing restores body scrolling and focus to the opening image link. Modified clicks retain normal browser behavior. Static sidecar previews do not simulate modal behavior.

CSV worksheet links are ordinary anchors with `download`, explicit file-type labels, a blue outline, rounded corners and a 48px minimum height. They become full-width on mobile and need no JavaScript.

### Contextual service next step

The closing service block uses navy, a white heading, and light blue supporting text and link. It has 16px corners, desktop padding 30px 32px and mobile padding 24px; its heading is 29px, reduced to 26px on mobile. It explains the specific unresolved task and preparation needed, then links to analytics setup or site optimization. Relevant services also appear as ordinary inline links in the steps, including analytics, usability audit, copywriting and optimization. Preserve contextual destinations rather than substituting a generic contact prompt. The block prints with black text and a border. Service-click enhancement emits an interaction event with inline/end placement; it does not demonstrate collected analytics or conversions.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** preserve the existing Optimism logo, Joanna family and navy/cyan identity within the blog.
- **Do** keep the text column bounded and let illustrations and contents reflow at the observed breakpoints.
- **Do** preserve visible focus, labeled controls, active states, reduced-motion behavior and the static reading path.
- **Do** distinguish editorial metadata, source screenshots and teaching examples from client results.
- **Do** keep source-image links, CSV downloads and contextual service links usable without JavaScript.

### Don't:

- **Don't** apply this scoped blog document as authority to redesign the homepage.
- **Don't** turn the open story entries into raised panels or add decorative shadows to reading surfaces.
- **Don't** treat the current two-entry arrangement, its topic counts or its specific copy as a permanent site-wide rule.
- **Don't** promote SVG-specific Arial labels or illustration-only tints into a new global type or color system.
- **Don't** restore decorative article hero covers merely because catalogue covers remain.
